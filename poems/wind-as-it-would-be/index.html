wind as it would be

by Bob Corrigan  ·  February 25, 2026

i. it fills space that needs it due to pressure systems. always escaping. howling. raging. winding away, wispy making leaves flutter symphonically on the trees. ii. sometimes I place myself all the way out in space. wind doesn't feel the same up there, and it's quiet. no space suit. Just a safe soul somehow, floating and I can't really even see a star or a planet, all so far away that I am surrounded by the dark. the brightest dark. and this is how the wind would be, staying steadily up above, pushing all the way to space so everything shrinks down a bit, and the distance wants to fold on itself. collapsed. iii. gentle pushes to continue to chase, and cheer, and flee the scene when I'm up there. In space. just being an imaginary space man I think, a little wind, would be nice.
