taking

by Robert Corrigan  ·  October 28, 2026

the tip of each toe, the grain of salt falling below. a wince when the sailor smoked again when he found the shore. it's still okay to find solace in meaning and the cleansing search within. whenever you actually take the time to try, anything. once. it's fine to say no. the link between the finite and the immediate vacuum above is just breath. our air, it's just the blanket from melting, popping, and exploding. it's just the soul itself: separate. connected.
