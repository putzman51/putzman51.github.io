tempered perihelion

by Bob Corrigan  ·  March 5, 2026

there's a time each day when golden orange hues streak through a vertically bound sea; beams held in suspended tension. with so many others around, pretending to look elsewhere, while you do too. it doesn't feel crowded now. hiding together while the planets keep pulling themselves into position.
