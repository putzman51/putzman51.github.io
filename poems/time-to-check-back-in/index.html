time to check back in

by Robert Corrigan  ·  April 23, 2026

scrunching every last vapor of thought into a small greyish black box that's hidden at the base of a tree. I'm still in space. The tree is in space too. The atmosphere is hugging the ground, surrounding the leaves individually as they rustle lightly tussled by it. it's just a thought silky black ribbons of vapor slinking silently through space-time. as they escape every attempt of capture, the celestial hand waving through it and sending it scattering into the vacuum. my dot (me) floating as a pin, a point, a person. zips through the vapor pushes through to deep space in seventeen seconds. a few deep breaths and my dot is: way out. a few more breaths and I'm: - further - fine - untethered - uncoiled - firm - closer - kind i might be a dot a small point sitting flush against the floor taking a breath and feeling the air freely escaping it there's a network of air in the sky
