steady through breath

by Robert Corrigan  ·  March 8, 2026

almost afraid to engage it even though you know it works it's waiting, it's placing it right behind your path reaching back a little more knowing it's there slow to setup the brace slow to start the swing moving in, smoothing in letting it happen as you know it should and you watch it as you live it there's a pop when it's right. you know it happened, and it's not pretend. explosion directed precisely practiced and patient it's okay what happens because I can watch it. the flight is not the freedom. The freedom is a steady throughline of you.
