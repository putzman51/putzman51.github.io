excited to see you

by Bob Corrigan  ·  March 16, 2026

hanging heavy, leaning back, rocking sitting and staring. talking in my head, floating, like a hover, skimming the surface quickly. blades of grass sitting still and whizzing past, the ground is still, yet escapes underneath. melting and falling heavier against the seat; I start drifting across side to side. it's a bit of a blur. wind is warm, ground is cold, one of those days where it's not as crisp as it should be. toes wiggle and eyes stay closed a little.
