hit

by Robert Corrigan  ·  March 3, 2026

i. (injury) there's a feeling – a concussion, where it feels like a roll of pennies just exists inside your face, and it's spreading around your head, and everything tastes like pennies. and it feels like the pennies are being pushed into your brain, and your eyes, and the whole thing is disorienting, because you're disoriented, and eventually you wake up and are told you cannot go back to sleep. ii. (flower) the rose; silently climbing. patiently grows, its scent, up close, felt immediately in the frontal lobe. a pillow of petals, stuffed suspiciously so, wanting to show off each little roseleaf. blooms becoming aware together and tangled around trellis they smell like you think the vine tastes. iii. (kiss) start inching from miles away. sly squinting and resisting. the pressure immeasurable, pushing petal to petal.
