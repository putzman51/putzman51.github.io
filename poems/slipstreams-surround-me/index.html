slipstreams surround me

by Robert Corrigan  ·  February 22, 2026

there's this whole, elucidated lightness (thing) after taking a sip of Hi-C that is poured into a fragile, impossibly weightless picnic cup, from a one point six-six liter can. the homeroom mom fills it, and the stream seems precarious, as the weight of the fluid is almost tipping the cup over, especially that first pour. before they were filled we held them loosely between middle finger + thumb in one hand so careful not to break it but flicking it upward to watch it flip as if that was the game, and it was, but we needed it to stay intact if we wanted it full, and, there's this whole "lightness" thing again. which feels heavy. told not to flick it, we did anyway because we need to feel as light as the cup so I look around, and pretend to, giggling, because I need to know that gravity doesn't apply here and I don't want to break it, eyes widen while we almost make each other laugh.
