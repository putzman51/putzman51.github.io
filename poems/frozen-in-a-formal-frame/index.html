frozen in a formal frame

by Bob Corrigan  ·  November 15, 2026

dryness, the smell of sand. a spice compared to arid air, or ground water engulfed soil. temperate touchpoints. lost in a way, an unknown waypoint. pointing back at the map where I know. following the distant pulses of electric signals, lighthouse-like in essence, but showing where sheer boredom lives. warning where the shore is. imagine heading right for it, the impulses guiding you away. the lighthouse itself, saying “don't find yourself here” not all dry ground has that spicey smell. not all wet ground gives a musty aroma either. lighting rods don't need to catch fire “let's find the time” doesn't need to result in a lost watch eyes tell the story. the ring around the center and the striations and gradients. when hues are beguiling, you'll know, like when you laugh at a joke. who knows what's funny who knows how to fall in love. locked in a look finding real time wrangling and sought after seeing there's no way you can lie right now I'm still alone in this.
