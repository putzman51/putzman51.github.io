every city is mine

by Bob Corrigan  ·  March 6, 2026

gummed up pavement pushing itself all the way out, until the strip malls start and the farmland sits tended nearby. the corridor connections busy between each dot, traffic tracing gentle lines translating to its true signature. it's the smell; everywhere. I own it. The way it's crisp or verdant or still yesterday's trash, that's me. the book stapled, says "atlas", folded and frayed, taking orders as much as giving them. making it fun to take another new turn. the sky is new here. the light, changed. waking up without knowing north feels better than I ever thought it would.
