there's a balloon

by Robert Corrigan  ·  October 26, 2026

there's air, a push of a breathy kiss coming from the side and pushing an apex across the top of the lake. not a wave but like a little mountain with a glacier, a snowy cap on the undulating peak. there's lift, a pencil being seen, felt, and experienced for what it really is. the writer knows this pencil will cure the soul. the reader never saw the pencil, but the graphite shavings were still felt. still, outside there's dirt. it's never really imposing, you don't have to engage. but this dirt is yours. The one god-given right to get dirty. it's your dirt. the balloon might bust, but it usually doesn't. the helium might help it get trapped in the rafters, bumping up against each ceiling tile. greying and yellowed light taking its time to enter into the middle of the ball. the air, somewhat stale, getting pushed. in a much more simple fashion than back at the lake. a slight arc, escaping behind as it moves like a blimp, unimposing but looming like a ghoul.
