it's enough

by Bob Corrigan  ·  October 28, 2026

to sit and wait through each idea and dream. frizzled bands of counted claims, speckled with a chromatic silken and sparkled silt. evaporating each time you blink. if you thought the fairness would settle your stomach, think again, it's really a stomach that keeps you from seeing anything fair. the time it takes to breathe is the life you have left at any given spark.
