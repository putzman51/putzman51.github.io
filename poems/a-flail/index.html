a flail

by Bob Corrigan  ·  October 30, 2026

placement in a state of mind that produces the simple treasure of slick ribbons tied inside and whipping the wind flapping and sometimes finding a groove and getting caught clouds are making darkness and damp sidewalks are not keeping the ribbons dry. it's, a fight. the strands are unraveling and the fray is now showing multiples of what was.
