screened in

by Bob Corrigan  ·  October 29, 2026

trusting the amount of scrutiny that goes into choosing one true green. the shades are going from “forest on fire” to “money you lost at 15” and back to “broccoli farmers’ weathered hands” and you just need “time to enjoy Christmas morning without all the fuss but the same energy as the day the tree arrived and the gift you gave them actually landed, and it wasn’t you just projecting” so you wind up with “tea green supreme” and call it a day. It’s never the exact right tone, and you will learn to love the reckless thoughts that float through as the floorboards absorb a little runoff.
