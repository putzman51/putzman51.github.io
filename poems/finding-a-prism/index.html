finding a prism

by Bob Corrigan  ·  March 12, 2026

they appear, softly, intentionally, because the light runs through water first, then finds a dancefloor. landing on a plate, perhaps in the sink because a mellow glow of orange escapes the stove. the prism waits to be discovered, an oblong green hugs the left; the spectrum follows. complete. it sits. complete, it is. filtered "cheap" stovetop light now glittered, free, rainbowed, gold.
← glowing in the dark steady through breath →
