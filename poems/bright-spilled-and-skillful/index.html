falling spoon  ·  all poems

bright, spilled, and skillful

by Bob Corrigan  ·  November 6, 2026

clouds and mist sifted. tree top filtered. green climbing up into the air thinned out by mountain breadth. eyes capture what the mind needs and knows the hourglass can forget. so the reverence must be made prominent. in this exact second. fleeting like a flock of heron floating with necks long. crying out as they fly saying “we know where we go” the darting sun, taking timber slowly upward. puzzled deer trying to hop past while learning to trot. enough air for the day. let’s get inside. starting to feel the next state over, the wind carrying leaves from miles to the west. the puddles start to look like you can pick them up with one hand. the moment is now for the rain is coming and going depending on how you made your move. enough tromping around in these boots.
