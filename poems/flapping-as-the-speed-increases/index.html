flapping as the speed increases

by Bob Corrigan  ·  November 4, 2026

sitting on one side the length extends past the point of barely being noticed. curled in a way, that seems accidental. timed with the trees, and taking each pocket of the vacuum and pushing back when the breeze increases. unraveling, aging, a patina of intention. keeping pace with the letter of the law. letting mathematics become the end result. every variable giving a ribbon of attrition. tied together by every thought. singing a song of tapping.
