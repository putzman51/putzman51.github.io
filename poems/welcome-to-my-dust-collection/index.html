welcome to my dust collection

by Bob Corrigan  ·  November 11, 2026

the breath. it moves dust, creates it, combining everything I shed. fine clumps finding other bunches of fuzzy investments, of time of life, progress is made with dust. letting it gather seeing its stale grey hue giving off an essence of insecure involvement. as in, “maybe I’m here for you to see moments move with milestones.” it’s not a pest. it’s not a chore. it’s the hourglass and the sand not trapped inside, but glittered around us like a snowglobe going nuts.
