a cold curling wind

by Bob Corrigan  ·  November 5, 2026

tall, thick, lopsided blades of grass coastal refuge a breeze that wouldn’t stop unless the waves told the wind “hey you, stop it for a second.” knowing the waves won’t stop a pulsing wind pulls a cloud past the shore. a crisp, chilled skin tone kissed with red, drying from the surface, a wetness in the air, staying still. rocks growing colder and all that makes sense is seeing sunshine and skipping in storms. sun still works when it’s behind something, when it wants to give you a break, when a ray of it pokes holes in the weather. cold wind whipping, sun rays sifting through time, pushing each wave while the timeless grass says goodbye.
