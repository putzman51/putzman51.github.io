sun-based sentience

by Robert Corrigan  ·  February 28, 2026

the fibers of the rug outside have collected dirt. Settling and softening. the sun has soaked itself especially in the upper right corner, next to her spot, she sips chai tea and daydreams about dances that don't stop and jokes that build worlds around them. her tiny sigh slips through, discovering a moment of rest in the slightly faded corner of the rug. you'd have to get low to really see the dirt. the sun sits angled, it only comes in a little, arriving through windows of branches submerging her space in its light.
