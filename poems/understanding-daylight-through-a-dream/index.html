understanding daylight through a dream

by Robert Corrigan  ·  March 1, 2026

the sun pushes effortlessly, the trees block what they can. light settles intently & splays itself on leaves, moving them slightly. each cloud i see around, bases its dreams on me. drifting into position, making sure i see, a radiant garden, sunshine glowing gold, lush earth grasping my heart. where smiles are shared; my dreams chase, each cloud i see around.
