the point is (me): occupied, reflected

by Robert Corrigan  ·  March 7, 2026

quick trip to vertically positioned consciousness up, up, way up. Take myself to a star, and keep going. I'm there. It's quiet. Dark. an ocean seems tiny up here, the expanse filled with all the thoughts that were ever released. it's dark, but I can see my hand. I stay long enough to cycle through a breath or two, inside my soul the vacuum pulls at me, pushes, waits for me to make a move a wiggle in sand, I'm a leaf riding the top of a river, in a celestine carnival. I come up to listen. I come up to think. I float away knowing, the overview.
