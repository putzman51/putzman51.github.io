they are not hiding once found out

by Robert Corrigan  ·  October 25, 2026

a hazy upstairs vestibule an intruder looking around ruffling, rifling through a drawer, glibly though seeking more than information, it's really just the excitement of the hunt the breaking in was one part yay. exciting. being found was the whole point. being seen, even through a hazy dream where the intruder is really there, but they know it's a dream and I don't yet. hiding. scanning, looking, seeking “when are they going to notice me” so, I call out. “hey!” they answer, calling back when hollered to from atop the stairs. wanting to be caught but still fighting back once the righteous are willing to remove. stifled screams from their fear of what might be. strength is summoned and the fight feels more like a cuddle because it's just a haze. they really just wanted to cuddle.
